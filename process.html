Process

How I build a retention flow.

Every flow starts with the customer’s hesitation and ends with a clear next step. Here is the order I work in.

Understand the brand and the buyer

Price, product, objections and how long the product lasts. This decides what each email has to answer and when the reorder should land.

Set up the foundation

Klaviyo account, domain authentication, list structure and sign-up forms, so every email reaches the inbox and every subscriber lands in the right place.

Map the lifecycle

Choose the trigger, timing, branches and exits. A customer should sit in one flow at a time, and in one language only.

Give each email one job

Deliver the code, answer the objection, explain the product, ask for the review, prompt the reorder. Then write and design it to match the brand.

Build and check

Set up splits and exit rules in Klaviyo, then test links, branches and the mobile view before anything goes live.

Test and refine

A/B test one change at a time: form offers, subject lines, send times and flow timing. Keep what works and drop what does not.
