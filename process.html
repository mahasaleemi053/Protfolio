Process

How I build a retention system.

Every flow starts with the buyer’s hesitation and ends with a clear next step. This is the order I work in.

01

Know the buyer

Price, product, objections and how long the product lasts.

02

Set the foundation

Account, domain authentication, list structure and sign-up forms.

03

Map the lifecycle

Triggers, timing, branches and exits. One flow and one language at a time.

04

One job per email

Deliver, answer, explain, ask, remind. Then write and design to match the brand.

05

Build and check

Splits, exit rules, links and mobile view, tested before anything goes out.

06

Test and refine

A/B test one change at a time and keep what works.

The thinking behind each step

  • Know the buyer

    What stops this person from buying? For Marlow it was price and dose; for Lumen D it was the product itself. That decides what every email must answer.

  • Set the foundation

    Authenticated domains, clean lists and well-built forms come first, so emails reach the inbox and subscribers land in the right flow.

  • Map the lifecycle

    A new buyer, a returning buyer and a non-buyer need different messages. I split them and set exits so nobody gets the wrong email, or the same email in two languages.

  • One job per email

    Each email earns its place: the code, the objection, the science, the review, the reorder. If it has no job, it is cut.

  • Build and check

    I build the branches in Klaviyo, then check every link, split and mobile view before anything goes live.

  • Test and refine

    Offers, subject lines, send times and flow timing are tested one change at a time, so I know what moved the result.
