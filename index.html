[CLINICAL REALITY]

The real leak isn't traffic, or even the consult.
It's the words around it.

Aesthetic patients are uniquely skeptical. They have been burned by 10-step skincare routines that wrecked their barrier, rushed 5-minute doctor visits, and MedSpa upselling.

When they step into your clinic, their guard is up. If your pre-consult copy doesn't validate their past disappointments, explain the exact treatment mechanism, and disarm their fear of high-pressure sales, they will leave your consultation room without booking.

CAMPAIGN SPECIFICATION

Adult Acne Multi-Session Treatment Program (Meta Ads)

Offer: $150–$350 Single / $600–$1,200 Package
Client Brief:

Direct-response ad scripts for a $150–$350/session (or $600–$1,200 package) medical-grade acne treatment program, targeting adults 20–30 disillusioned with rushed dermatology visits, MedSpa upselling, and failed DIY skincare routines.

Strategic Approach:

Five scripts were built around five distinct entry points into audience skepticism, each engineered to disarm a specific objection before introducing the offer, then adapted to a first-person dermatologist POV to increase perceived medical authority and trust.

AD 01 Audience: Cold to warm

"The Five Minute Visit"

Angle: Dermatologist disillusionment · Pain point: Rushed clinical visits, lack of education on side effects

HOOK (0:00-0:03)

"I used to give patients five minutes and a prescription. I don't do that anymore."

PROBLEM (0:03-0:18)

"Early in my career, that's what a packed schedule looked like. Write the script, move to the next room, hope the patient figured out the rest on their own. I'd hear back weeks later that their skin got worse before anyone explained purging was normal. That's not good medicine, it's just fast medicine."

UNIQUE ANGLE (0:18-0:32)

"Now I sit down with every patient and walk through why their acne keeps returning before I treat anything. We build a six to eight week plan that adjusts as your skin responds, not a script you fill and figure out alone."

CALL TO ACTION (0:32-0:42)

"If your last dermatologist visit felt rushed, come see us. I'll actually explain what's happening to your skin before we start treating it."

Why It Works: Reframes a common failure point (the rushed appointment) as something the speaker used to do and has since corrected, which builds credibility through admitted imperfection rather than a polished sales claim.
AD 02 Audience: Cold

"The Upsell"

Angle: Zero-upsell trust guarantee · Pain point: Fear of high-pressure sales, distrust of MedSpa pitches

HOOK (0:00-0:03)

"I'm a dermatologist, and I will not point at your pores to sell you a product line."

PROBLEM (0:03-0:16)

"I've had patients come to me after MedSpa visits where an esthetician listed everything wrong with their skin, then handed them a six hundred dollar shopping list. That's not treatment. That's a sales pitch wearing a lab coat."

UNIQUE ANGLE (0:16-0:30)

"In my practice, we don't do that. No upsells during your session. We build your treatment plan around your skin, and we work with whatever's already in your routine that's actually helping."

CALL TO ACTION (0:30-0:40)

"Book a consultation and see how a session runs without the pitch at the end."

Why It Works: Names the exact mechanism of past exploitation (pointing out flaws to sell product) rather than a vague promise of "honesty," which makes the guarantee feel specific and earned.
AD 03 Audience: Warm

"Still Breaking Out at 26"

Angle: Age-based frustration · Pain point: Belief that adult acne is permanent or untreatable

HOOK (0:00-0:03)

"Patients ask me constantly, why am I still breaking out at 26?"

PROBLEM (0:03-0:17)

"Adult acne isn't rare, and it's almost never bad luck. Most of the patients I see have spent years stacking cleansers, serums, and prescriptions on top of each other without anyone explaining what their skin actually needed."

UNIQUE ANGLE (0:17-0:31)

"In most cases, the real issue is a damaged skin barrier from years of harsh products layered together. My program spends the first two weeks calming that barrier before we introduce active treatment at all, which is why results tend to hold instead of relapsing."

CALL TO ACTION (0:31-0:41)

"If that sounds like your skin, book a consultation with me. Link below."

Why It Works: Externalizes blame away from the patient ("it's almost never bad luck") while still giving a concrete physiological explanation, which relieves shame without sounding dismissive of the real damage caused by past products.
AD 04 Audience: Warm to retargeted

"The Mirror Check"

Angle: Emotional self-consciousness · Pain point: Mental exhaustion of constant self-monitoring

HOOK (0:00-0:03)

"The patients I see aren't chasing perfect skin. They're exhausted."

PROBLEM (0:03-0:19)

"Most of them describe the same thing to me. Checking their skin in every mirror they pass, covering up before video calls, second-guessing a photo before they post it. That mental weight is often harder on someone than the acne itself."

UNIQUE ANGLE (0:19-0:33)

"I'm not going to promise you flawless skin in six weeks. What I can build is a structured plan that calms your skin enough that you stop thinking about it constantly, which is usually what people actually came in for."

CALL TO ACTION (0:33-0:43)

"If you're ready to stop checking the mirror, book your first session with me."

Why It Works: Explicitly rejects the "flawless skin" promise this audience has learned to distrust, then replaces it with the actual outcome they're chasing (mental relief), which research showed matters more to this group than cosmetic perfection.
AD 05 Audience: Cold, high purchase intent

"Before You Try Another Serum"

Angle: Comparison shopping / price objection · Pain point: Outcome uncertainty disguised as price hesitation

HOOK (0:00-0:03)

"Before you buy another forty dollar serum, I want to tell you something as a dermatologist."

PROBLEM (0:03-0:18)

"I see patients who've spent more treating their acne piecemeal than they realize. A cleanser here, a serum there, a rushed dermatology visit that never really addressed the cause. It adds up, and none of it answers why the breakouts keep coming back."

UNIQUE ANGLE (0:18-0:32)

"A single session with me runs $150 to $350. If you decide to continue into the full program, that cost applies directly to your package. Nothing wasted while you figure out whether it's right for you."

CALL TO ACTION (0:32-0:42)

"Book your first session and I'll walk you through an actual plan for your skin. Link below."

Why It Works: Addresses the price objection by reframing it against cumulative spending on failed products rather than defending the price itself, and removes financial risk with the credit-rollover structure instead of relying on urgency or scarcity.
CAMPAIGN BRIEF & STRATEGY

Clearwell Dermatology — 7-Email Acne Program Sequence

Client: Clearwell Dermatology (professional skincare / medical aesthetics). Audience: Warm traffic, adults 20–30, who've already cycled through OTC products, complex routines, and at least one disappointing derm or med spa visit — emotionally fatigued, skeptical of upsells, burned out on products that overpromised.

The sequence is built on a trust-before-offer arc rather than a traditional AIDA push. The first two emails validate the reader's specific failure pattern before any product is mentioned. The offer isn't introduced until Email 5, after the mechanism (Email 3) and proof (Email 4) have done the trust-building work. The zero-upsell guarantee is repeated across multiple emails deliberately, since past med spa upsell trauma was identified as the single biggest conversion blocker for this audience.

Primary CTA: Book a $199 diagnostic session (credited toward full package)
Select Email (1–7):
EMAIL 01 // AWARENESS

Subject: the thing nobody tells you about adult acne

Preview: it's not your fault, and it's not your hygiene

Copywriter's Note (Portfolio Context): This sequence intentionally withholds the offer until Email 5, using the first four emails to build the specific kind of trust this audience needs before they'll consider spending $600+. The zero-upsell guarantee appears three separate times across the sequence rather than once — a deliberate choice based on audience research showing med spa upsell trauma as the top conversion blocker. Proof in Email 4 is structured as a documented timeline rather than a testimonial quote, since process-based proof outperformed generic praise for this specific audience.
VIDEO SALES LETTER SCRIPT

"The Skin Barrier Truth" — Full 11-Beat VSL

Built around barrier-repair positioning and a zero-upsell guarantee to convert skeptical, treatment-fatigued adults who've been burned by rushed dermatologist visits and pushy MedSpa upsells.

Target Duration: 6–9 Minutes
BEAT 01 · HOOK

"If you've spent the last few years trying every acne product on the internet and your skin still isn't cooperating, I want you to stop for a second and consider something you probably haven't heard from a dermatologist, an esthetician, or anyone trying to sell you something."

"Your skin isn't broken. It's been mistreated. Not by you. By the routine you were told to follow."

BEAT 02 · BIG PROMISE

"Over the next few minutes, I'm going to show you the exact protocol we use at Clearwell Dermatology to calm active breakouts, repair a damaged skin barrier, and get you to visibly clearer skin in 6 to 8 weeks. No ten-step routine. No product cabinet. No one pointing at your face telling you what else is wrong with it. Just a structured plan that treats your skin like an organ that needs healing, not a problem that needs punishing."

BEAT 03 · RELATABILITY

"You already know the morning routine. You reach up and check your jawline before you even open your eyes, hoping the bump from last night flattened out overnight. It didn't. It's still there, and now it's angrier than it was yesterday. You've bought the salicylic acid cleanser. You've bought the benzoyl peroxide spot treatment. Somewhere in your bathroom cabinet there's a half-used bottle of a Vitamin C serum that broke you out worse than when you started. You waited two months for a dermatology appointment, and the visit lasted five minutes. You walked out with a prescription and a "this might just be something you deal with." You went home, tried it anyway, and your face peeled for two weeks straight. Maybe you tried a MedSpa after that. Sat in the chair while someone pointed at your enlarged pores and told you the real fix was a four hundred dollar product suite you hadn't budgeted for. You left feeling worse about your skin than when you walked in. If any of that sounds familiar, you're not the exception. You're the pattern."

BEAT 04 · PROBLEM AGITATION

"Here's what nobody explains to you in any of those five minute appointments. Every one of those failed attempts did the same thing to your skin. It stripped the barrier. Salicylic acid, benzoyl peroxide, physical scrubs, aggressive retinoids. Used on their own in moderation, they can help. Stacked on top of each other the way most routines end up, they strip your stratum corneum, dry out your skin, and trigger your glands to overproduce oil to compensate. That oil clogs your pores. Which causes more breakouts. Which sends you looking for something even stronger. You're not failing at skincare. You're stuck in a loop that was never going to end on its own. And underneath the physical cycle sits something heavier. You're twenty five, twenty eight, thirty, and you assumed this was a teenage problem you'd outgrow. Instead you're sitting in a work meeting wondering if your manager is looking at the cyst on your chin instead of listening to what you're saying. None of that is vanity. It's exhausting to carry, and it's real."

BEAT 05 · NEW OPPORTUNITY

"So here's where things shift. The problem was never that your skin is uniquely difficult, or that you're doing something wrong every morning. The problem is that almost everything sold to treat acne attacks the surface without repairing what's underneath it. At Clearwell, we start from a different assumption. Before we introduce anything new to your skin, we rebuild the barrier that's been stripped by everything you've already tried. Calm first. Then clarity. That order matters more than most people realize, and it's the piece that gets skipped in a rushed dermatology visit or a product-driven MedSpa consultation."

BEAT 06 · MECHANISM EXPLANATION

"The program runs across three phases over six to eight weeks. Weeks one and two are about calming active inflammation and repairing your moisture barrier, using gentle LED light therapy and hydration-focused facials, paired with a simplified two-step home routine. Weeks three through five move into targeted treatment — once your barrier is more stable, we introduce mild light therapy or IPL alongside controlled extractions and prescription topicals, calibrated to clear congestion without undoing the repair work from phase one. Weeks six through eight focus on stabilization, addressing post-inflammatory marks, evening out sebum production, and setting you up with a maintenance plan you can actually sustain. And this next part is the one thing most people ask about first, so let's address it directly. We do not sell product lines. We call it our Zero-Upsell Guarantee."

BEAT 07 · PROOF

"We track results the same way every time. Photos at week zero, week two, week four, week six, and week eight, same lighting, same angle, so you can actually see the progression instead of guessing at it. One client came in at twenty six after two failed dermatology prescriptions and a MedSpa membership she regretted signing. By week four her active cystic breakouts had visibly reduced. By week eight, her words to us were that she stopped needing full coverage foundation to leave the house. That's the outcome we're actually building toward. Not airbrushed skin. The ability to stop thinking about your face every time you walk into a room."

BEAT 08 · OFFER REVEAL

"Here's how we've structured this so you're not making a twelve hundred dollar decision on blind trust. You start with a single diagnostic session, priced between $150 and $350 depending on your specific treatment plan. This is a full clinical assessment under magnification, plus your first calming treatment. If you decide to continue into the full 6-to-8-week protocol, that entire session fee is credited, dollar for dollar, toward your package. The full program runs $600 to $1,200 for four to six sessions, depending on the treatment mix your skin needs. You're never locked into the full program on day one. You're trying it first."

BEAT 09 · BONUSES

"Every client who books their diagnostic session this month also receives: a pre-consultation intake review, so your first session isn't spent re-explaining everything you've already tried; a written barrier-repair home guide, the simplified two-step routine designed to work alongside your in-clinic sessions; and direct message access to your provider between sessions three and six, for the weeks where you have a question and don't want to wait until your next appointment to ask it."

BEAT 10 · URGENCY

"We intentionally keep our client load small enough that every session is done directly with a provider, not handed off to rotating staff. That means diagnostic session slots are limited each month. If you're dealing with an event, a season, or a stretch of your life where you want your skin stabilized rather than getting worse while you wait, the sooner you start the six to eight week window, the sooner you're through it."

BEAT 11 · CALL TO ACTION

"If you're tired of starting over. If you're tired of routines that promise everything and deliver a peeling face and a bill for products you didn't ask for, book your diagnostic session below. No product pitch. No membership pressure. Just an honest look at your skin and a plan built around fixing what's actually happening underneath it. Click the link below to book your $150 to $350 diagnostic session at Clearwell Dermatology. Your fee rolls over completely if you continue. Your skin doesn't have to stay stuck in the same loop it's been in for years."
