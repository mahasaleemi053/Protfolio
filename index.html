My mindset

Four rules behind every flow.

01

Answer the real objection

Every flow starts with what stops this buyer: price, dose, trust or timing. The emails exist to remove it.

02

One job per email

Deliver, explain, answer, ask or remind. An email with no job gets cut.

03

One customer, one flow

Splits and exits keep each person in the right flow, at the right stage, in the right language.

04

Test one change at a time

Offers, subject lines and timing are tested separately, so I know what actually moved.
